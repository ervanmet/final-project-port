About Me

Hello! My name is Eve VanMeter and I am a Media Arts and Science student specializing in photography and visual storytelling, with a focus on capturing authentic human connection. My work centers on photographing people through intentional composition, lighting, and mood to create images that feel natural, emotionally engaging, and unposed. I’m drawn to real moments and genuine expression, paying attention to the small details that make each person unique. Whether I’m shooting portraits, candid moments, or events, I prioritize creating a comfortable environment so personality can emerge naturally in front of the camera. As I continue developing my skills in photography and digital media, I seek opportunities that emphasize storytelling, connection, and meaningful visual narratives. My goal is to create work that feels honest, collaborative, and visually compelling.
